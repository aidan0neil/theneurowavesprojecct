Meet the Team photos
====================

Add photos from your Meet the Team Instagram post here.

Filenames used on the Meet the Team page (public/gallery.html):
  person1.jpg  person2.jpg  person3.jpg  person4.jpg

Steps:
1. Save the photos from your Instagram post.
2. Put them here with the names above (or edit gallery.html to use different names).
3. In public/gallery.html, replace each placeholder "Name", "Role", and "Short bio from your Instagram post." with the real name, role, and bio for that person.
4. Add or remove <article class="team-card"> blocks in gallery.html if you have more or fewer than 4 team members.
